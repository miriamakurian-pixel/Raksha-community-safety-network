Quick Safety References & Protocol

Essential guidance for night-shift commutes, emergency de-escalation, and legal rights in India.

Zero FIR Rights (India)

Under Indian Law, a **Zero FIR** can be lodged at any police station regardless of jurisdiction or place of incident occurrence. Police officers are legally bound to register it and forward it to the concerned precinct without delay.

Street De-escalation Protocol

If followed, change path toward illuminated commercial hubs or safe houses. Maintain firm posture, keep phone accessible, and broadcast live GPS using the SOS trigger to alert your designated emergency contacts immediately.

Safe House Host Guidelines

Verified hosts must provide well-lit entrance areas, basic first-aid supplies, telephone access, and shelter until emergency contacts or local police arrive to accompany the member home.

Commute Buddy Code of Conduct

Buddies must meet strictly at agreed converging points. Always confirm arrival using the dual "I Met My Buddy" button to update active route statuses and earn system safety credits.
