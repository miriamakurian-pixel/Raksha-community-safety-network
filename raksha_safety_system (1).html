Quick Reference, Safety Protocols & Emergency Guides

Essential procedures, de-escalation methods, safe house operational manuals, and legal protections for late-night commuters.

1. Emergency Code Words & SOS

  • "Code Red Lotus": Universal verbal cue when in immediate danger at a safe house or with a walk companion.
  • Rapid SOS Dispatch: Pressing the red dashboard SOS button sends automated GPS alert SMS messages to both Emergency Contacts.

2. Walk Buddy Guidelines

  • Converging Rendezvous: Always meet at well-lit public converging locations assigned on your map.
  • Dual Confirmation: Both users click "I Met My Buddy" to confirm identity & lock +5 Safety Points.

3. Safe House Volunteer Manual

  • Physical Verification Audit: Safe houses undergo a physical safety inspection by a Raksha volunteer prior to going active.
  • Temporary Harbor: Provide a safe, lit space until trusted transport or local authorities arrive.

4. De-escalation Techniques

  • Maintain Distance: Keep a 6-foot barrier between yourself and unknown suspicious parties.
  • Voice Projection: Speak firmly and loudly: "Back away immediately" to draw public attention.

5. Legal Rights & Police Support

  • Zero FIR Right: Women can file a Zero FIR at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction location.
  • Sunset Protection: Women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except under exceptional magistrate orders.

6. Community Ethics

  • Data Privacy: Phone numbers and exact addresses are masked and accessible only to verified moderators.
  • Mutual Respect: Maintain supportive, non-intrusive communication during walk companion sessions.
